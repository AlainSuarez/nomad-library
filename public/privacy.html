Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

  • We never ask for your name, phone number or a password. Giving us your email is optional, and it's only used for the emails you sign up for.
  • There are no ads and no tracking. We keep one anonymous number: how many devices open the site each day.
  • We don't sell or share anything with anyone, your email included.
  • You can use the whole site without an account. Then nothing about you is stored on our servers.
  • If you make an account, we keep only your library, favorites, watchlist and settings, so they follow you to other devices. You can delete all of it in one tap.

If you don't make an account

Your library choice, favorites and settings are saved in your own browser's storage on your device. They never reach our servers. Clearing your browser's data removes them.

If you make an account

Accounts use passkeys, which means you sign in with your face, fingerprint or device PIN. Your face and fingerprint never leave your device, and we never see them. Here's everything we store for an account:

  • A random account number.
  • Your passkey's public key, which lets us check that it's you. It can't be used to sign in anywhere else.
  • A scrambled copy of your backup code. We can't read the original.
  • The library you picked, your favorites, your imported watchlist titles and your display settings.
  • The dates your account was made and last updated.

That's it. There's no email, name, location or payment information attached to an account.

If you give us your email

You can give us your email in 2 places, and both are optional:

  • "Tell me when my library is ready." If we can't show your library's shelves yet, you can ask to be emailed when we can. We keep your email, the library's name and the catalog link you gave us, so we know which library you're waiting for.
  • Updates. If you sign up for updates, we may email you about new libraries and features, and possibly a newsletter in the future.

We keep only your email address, what you signed up for and the date. It isn't connected to an account, your favorites or anything else. Emails are sent through Google's Gmail service, so Google handles their delivery.

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. One tap deletes your email address from all our lists right away. You can also ask us to delete it through the channel listed under Questions below.

Counting visits

Once a day, your browser tells us "a device visited." That adds 1 to a daily total so we can see how many people use the site. It sends no name, ID or cookie, and we can't tell which device it was or connect it to anything else. We also count how many accounts exist.

Deleting your data

Open Account and choose Delete my account. Everything above is erased right away and can't be recovered. Signing out leaves a copy of your shelf on that device until you clear your browser's data.

Cookies

Nomad Library uses 1 cookie, and only if you sign in. It keeps you signed in on that device. While you're signing in, a second short-lived cookie holds the security check and expires within 10 minutes. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Your watchlist file

When you import an IMDb or Letterboxd watchlist, the file is read inside your browser. Only the movie titles and years are sent to look them up at your library. The file itself is never uploaded. If you're signed in, the titles and matches are saved to your account so they show up on your other devices.

Other services the site talks to

  • Your library's catalog. Searches go through our server to the library's catalog service (BiblioCommons), so the library sees our server's request, not you.
  • Cover images load straight from the cover service your library uses, the same way they do on the library's own website.
  • Trailers, IMDb, holds and tips. The Trailer, IMDb, Place a hold and Donate buttons open YouTube, IMDb, your library's website or Venmo. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting. The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver pages and block attacks. We don't use it to identify or follow anyone.

Kids

Nomad Library isn't aimed at children under 13. The only personal information it can collect is an email address someone chooses to give us, and children under 13 shouldn't sign up for emails.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.

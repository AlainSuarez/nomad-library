Your library's movies,
all on one shelf.

Browse every DVD and Blu-ray at your public library with ratings, trailers and what's on the shelf right now. It's free, and so is borrowing them.

On your library's website, open the catalog. If the address shows something.bibliocommons.com, paste that link here.

Bring your watchlist

Export your watchlist from IMDb or Letterboxd, drop the file here, and see which of those movies your library has.

How do I export it?

IMDb: open your Watchlist, tap the three dots, then Export. The file arrives in your Exports page.

Letterboxd: Settings, then Data, then Export your data. Unzip it and use watchlist.csv.
